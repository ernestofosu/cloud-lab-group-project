Frequently asked questions

GT Library is open to registered students and lecturers of the institution. Students can search, read and download approved resources; lecturers can additionally upload books, lecture notes, research papers and past examination papers.

Click Register and choose whether you're a student or a lecturer. Fill in your details and you'll be able to log in right away.

Every resource a lecturer uploads is reviewed by a library administrator before it appears in the public catalogue. This keeps the collection accurate, appropriate and well organised.

Yes. Every resource can be read online in the built-in PDF viewer. Downloading is only available when the lecturer who uploaded it has allowed downloads.

Go to the login page and select "Forgot password?". You'll receive a reset link by email if the address is registered.

Accounts are suspended by an administrator, usually after repeated policy violations or at the account holder's request. Contact the library team for details.
